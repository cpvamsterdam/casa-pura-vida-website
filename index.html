Our story

Space for everyone, right on the canal

Casa Pura Vida sits directly on the canals of Amsterdam, a real house that is built, not a boat. With 250 m² of living space and a 300 m² garden, it's built for groups, families and couples who want room to spread out without leaving the water behind. The house unfolds across two levels: the entrance floor holds the living room and kitchen, while the floor below is devoted entirely to rest, with all of the bedrooms and bathrooms.

  • Sleeps up to 12 guests across 4 bedrooms
  • Free parking for your car
  • 2 double kayaks + 10 bicycles free to use, including kids' bikes
  • 10 minutes from the airport, 10-20 minutes from central Amsterdam

Getting around

  • 10 minutes from the airport
  • 10-20 minutes from every central point in the city; average one-way taxi fare €15-20
  • The neighbourhood is rural, walk along the lakes and canals past the area's distinctive houses, or cycle into the forest and the neighbouring village of Badhoevedorp
  • Two supermarkets nearby, side by side: Albert Heijn and Lidl
  • A 4-minute bike ride or 15-minute walk to the tram stop for lines 1 and 2, reaching every major location in Amsterdam
